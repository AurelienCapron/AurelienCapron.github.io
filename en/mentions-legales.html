Local preferences and statistics

The site remembers language and theme choices in your browser’s local storage (“localStorage”, keys “lang” and “theme”), solely to restore your chosen presentation. Portfolio code does not send these values to a server. They have no programmed expiry and remain until changed or deleted, including by clearing site data in your browser settings.
No audience measurement tool, advertising pixel or cross-site tracking is enabled in this version. The portfolio therefore does not request advertising consent. Hosting security logs and the policies of sites opened through links are separate from these local preferences.
